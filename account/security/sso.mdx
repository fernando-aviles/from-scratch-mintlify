title: "Single sign-on (SSO)"
sidebarTitle: "Single sign-on"
description: "Let your team sign in to Relaybox with your company's identity provider."
---

Available on: <Badge color="purple">Enterprise</Badge>

With SSO, members sign in through your identity provider (IdP) instead of with a Relaybox password. When someone leaves your company and you deactivate them in your IdP, they lose access to Relaybox too.

Relaybox supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect.

## Set up SSO

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Okta">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create the app in Okta">
        In the Okta admin console, go to **Applications → Browse App Catalog**, search for **Relaybox**, and click **Add integration**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy the metadata URL">
        On the **Sign On** tab, copy the **Metadata URL**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect Relaybox">
        In Relaybox, go to **Settings → Security → Single sign-on**, choose **Okta**, and paste the metadata URL.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Microsoft Entra ID">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Register an enterprise application">
        In the Entra admin center, go to **Enterprise applications → New application → Create your own application**. Name it "Relaybox" and choose **Integrate any other application you don't find in the gallery**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Configure SAML">
        Under **Single sign-on**, choose **SAML**. Set the **Identifier** and **Reply URL** to the values shown in Relaybox under **Settings → Security → Single sign-on**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Upload the federation metadata">
        Download the **Federation Metadata XML** and upload it to Relaybox.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Workspace">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add a custom SAML app">
        In the Google Admin console, go to **Apps → Web and mobile apps → Add app → Add custom SAML app**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Exchange details">
        Download the IdP metadata from Google, then enter the ACS URL and Entity ID from Relaybox.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn it on">
        Set **User access** to **ON for everyone**, or for the organizational units that should have access.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Enforce SSO

After testing, turn on **Require SSO**. Members who signed up with a password will be asked to sign in through your IdP the next time they visit.

<Warning>
  The account owner can always sign in with a password and a second factor, so you can't be locked out if your IdP has an outage. Make sure the owner account uses a strong, unique password.
</Warning>

## Just-in-time provisioning

When **Just-in-time provisioning** is on, anyone your IdP lets through gets a Relaybox account automatically, with the **Viewer** role. Map IdP groups to roles to grant more:

```json
{
  "role_mappings": [
    { "idp_group": "platform-engineering", "role": "admin" },
    { "idp_group": "engineering", "role": "developer" }
  ]
}
```
