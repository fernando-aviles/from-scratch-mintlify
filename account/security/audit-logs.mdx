---
title: "Audit logs"
description: "A tamper-evident record of every change made to your Relaybox account."
---

Available on: <Badge color="green">Scale and Enterprise</Badge>

Audit logs record **who** did **what**, **when**, and **from where**. They cover changes made in the dashboard, through the API, and through the CLI.

## What's logged

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Endpoints">
    Created, updated, paused, resumed, deleted, and secret rotated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="API keys">
    Created, rotated, revoked, and scopes changed. The key itself is never logged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Team">
    Member invited, removed, and role changed. Ownership transferred.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Security settings">
    SSO configured or changed, Require SSO turned on or off, IP allowlist changed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replays">
    Who replayed which events, to which endpoints. Publishing events isn't an audited action, because it's already recorded in the event log.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Log entry format

```json
{
  "id": "aud_7Gm3Rk",
  "action": "endpoint.secret_rotated",
  "actor": {
    "type": "member",
    "email": "dana@example.com"
  },
  "target": {
    "type": "endpoint",
    "id": "ep_5Np8Wz"
  },
  "ip_address": "203.0.113.42",
  "occurred_at": "2026-10-18T09:15:44Z"
}
```

## Exporting

Download logs as CSV from **Settings → Audit log**, or stream them continuously to Datadog, Splunk, or an S3 bucket. Streaming destinations receive new entries within one minute.

<Info>
  Audit logs are retained for one year on every eligible plan, independent of your event retention.
</Info>
