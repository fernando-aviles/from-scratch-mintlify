## How usage is counted

You're billed per **event published**, not per delivery. An event fanned out to ten endpoints counts once. Retries and replays are free.

<Accordion title="Example: calculating a monthly bill">
  Suppose you're on the Team plan and publish 2.4 million events in a month:

  - 2 million events are included in the plan.
  - The remaining 400,000 events are billed at {overageRate}.
  - 400,000 ÷ 10,000 × $0.25 = **$10.00** in overage.

  Your total for the month is $49 + $10 = **$59.00**.
</Accordion>

## What happens at the limit?

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Hobby">
    On the Hobby plan, publishing stops when you reach 100,000 events. The API returns `429 rate_limited` until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade. Events you already published are still delivered.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Paid plans">
    Paid plans never stop publishing. Events above your included volume are billed at {overageRate}. You can set a **spend cap** under **Settings → Billing** to receive an alert, or to stop publishing, at a dollar amount you choose.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

<Note>
  If you downgrade to a plan with shorter log retention, logs older than the new limit are deleted when the downgrade takes effect. Export anything you need first.
</Note>
