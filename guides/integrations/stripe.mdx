## Why put Relaybox in front of Stripe?

Stripe retries failed webhooks for up to three days, but you can't easily replay a specific event to a specific service, and only one URL receives each event. With Relaybox in the middle:

- Every internal service gets its own endpoint and its own retry queue.
- You can replay any Stripe event during your retention window.
- Signature verification happens once, at the edge.

## Set up the source

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the source">
    Go to **Integrations → Stripe** and click **Create source**. Relaybox gives you an ingest URL like `https://in.relaybox.dev/src_9Kq2`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the URL in Stripe">
    In the Stripe dashboard, go to **Developers → Webhooks → Add endpoint**, paste the ingest URL, and select the events you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the Stripe signing secret">
    Stripe shows a signing secret for the new endpoint. Paste it back into Relaybox so that we can reject requests that didn't come from Stripe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Stripe events now appear in Relaybox with the type prefixed by `stripe.` — for example, `stripe.invoice.paid`.

<Warning>
  Your internal services will now receive **Relaybox** signatures, not Stripe signatures. Update them to verify with the Relaybox endpoint secret.
</Warning>
