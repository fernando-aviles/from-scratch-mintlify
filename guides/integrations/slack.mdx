  Send a formatted message to a channel whenever an event is published.
</IntegrationHeader>

## Connect your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authorize Relaybox">
    In the dashboard, go to **Integrations → Slack** and click **Add to Slack**. Choose the workspace and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel and events">
    Pick the channel to post in and the event types that should trigger a message. Private channels require you to invite the **Relaybox** app first by typing `/invite @Relaybox` in the channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the message">
    Use a template to control the message text. Templates use `{{ }}` placeholders that refer to fields in the event:

    ```handlebars
    :package: Order *{{payload.order_id}}* shipped to {{payload.shipping.city}}.
    <{{payload.tracking_url}}|Track the package>
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rate limits

Slack allows about one message per second per channel. If you publish faster than that, Relaybox queues messages and sends them as fast as Slack accepts them.

<Tip>
  For high-volume event types, consider a [transformation](/guides/sending-events/transformations) that only forwards the events a human actually needs to see.
</Tip>
